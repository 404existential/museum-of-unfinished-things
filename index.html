Institution Profile

We preserve the evidence of intention.

The Museum of Unfinished Things is a public digital institution created to hold work that stopped before its intended conclusion: a half-written novel, a startup that never launched, an unrecorded melody, an abandoned algorithm, an architectural blueprint without a building.

Nothing here is presented as cautionary, nor as a lesson in productivity or perseverance. The record itself is sufficient. In human creative life, the decision to stop is as genuine as the decision to begin.
