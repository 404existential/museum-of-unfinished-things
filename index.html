About the museum

We preserve the evidence of intention.

The Museum of Unfinished Things is a digital archive devoted to work that stopped before its intended conclusion.

A half-written novel. A prototype that never shipped. A business plan abandoned in a folder. A photograph that never became a series.

We do not treat these objects as failures. We simply document them. The circumstances surrounding an unfinished thing are often as interesting as the thing itself.
